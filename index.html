DEEP LOOKING

What do you see when you experience reality? Your experience is captured by an image or perhaps multiple images in a video. Within these artifacts, one can use multiple lenses for greater insight into looking. We define a lens as either personal (based on subjective knowledge) or formal (based on classes taken or formal classification systems such as Dewey Decimal or Library of Congress). We call this Deep Looking. As you go about your day, take time to reflect on what you are seeing and ask questions. Many questions and answers to questions are rooted in academic subjects (disciplines).

There are different lens selection styles in the gallery: (1) curated multi-lens, (2) LA MESH lenses, and (3) single lens.
