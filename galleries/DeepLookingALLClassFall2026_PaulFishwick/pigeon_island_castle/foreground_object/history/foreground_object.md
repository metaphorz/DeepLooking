The photographer was aboard a cruise ship in Kuşadası, a port on Turkey's Aegean coast, and the pale fixture in the foreground belongs to that harbor world. Ships have tied up in this area for centuries. The nearby site of Ephesus was a great ancient port until its harbor silted up, and trade then shifted to nearby coastal towns. The fortress on Pigeon Island dates mostly to the Ottoman period, around the 1500s and 1600s, when it guarded the sea approach. Today the island is joined to the mainland by a causeway. Where Ottoman merchant ships once docked, modern cruise liners now bring visitors to explore the same coast.
