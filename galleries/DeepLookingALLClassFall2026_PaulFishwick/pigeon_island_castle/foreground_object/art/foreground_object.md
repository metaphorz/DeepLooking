Artists often place a bright, large object at the edge of a picture to pull the viewer inward. Here the pale, rounded shape at the bottom right works that way. It is small, but it is the lightest spot in a scene of deep navy water and sky. It acts like a frame, and it makes the lit castle walls on the island seem much farther away. The dark empty water between them creates a sense of depth and quiet. The warm yellow of the walls and the cool blue of dusk form a color contrast that painters call warm against cool. The foreground object grounds the photographer in a real place on the ship.
