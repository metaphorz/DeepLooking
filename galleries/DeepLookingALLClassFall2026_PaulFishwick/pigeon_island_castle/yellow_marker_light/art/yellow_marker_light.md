This scene shows a classic color contrast. The small yellow marker glows against a deep blue evening sky and dark water, and yellow and blue sit near opposite sides of the color wheel, so each makes the other look stronger. Artists call this a focal point, a bright spot that pulls the eye first. Here the thin vertical shape also echoes the long horizontal lines of the pier and the sea, creating balance. The marker is placed on the right while the lit castle walls fill the left, so the two glowing areas answer each other across the dark water, like notes in a musical duet.
