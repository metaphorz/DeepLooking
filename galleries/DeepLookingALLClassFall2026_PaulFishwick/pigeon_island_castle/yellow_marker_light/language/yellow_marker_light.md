The glowing yellow marker at the end of the breakwater is a small beacon, and its name comes from Old English words for a signal or sign. A signal light is a good example of semiotics, the study of signs. A color and a flash pattern can carry a message without a single spoken word. Sailors read yellow lights as special marks, not as ordinary lamps. Nearby, the island's Turkish name, Güvercin Adası, means Pigeon Island. Güvercin is the word for pigeon, and ada means island. Harbor lights, charts, and names together form a shared language that lets ships from many countries enter a port safely.
