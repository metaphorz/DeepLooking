The castle on Pigeon Island is usually linked to the Byzantine and Ottoman eras. Its defensive walls were reinforced in the 1500s and 1600s, when the Ottomans needed protection against pirates and rival navies in the Aegean. Kuşadası was a trading port near Ephesus, so a lookout tower on the island helped guard shipping. Later the island lost its military role. A causeway was built, and the site became a park with cafes and a tourist landmark. Today a Turkish flag flies from the tower, which reminds visitors that the fortress has stayed part of the community's story for centuries.
