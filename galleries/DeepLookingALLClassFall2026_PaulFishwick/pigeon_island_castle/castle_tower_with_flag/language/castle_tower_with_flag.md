The town's name, Kuşadası, is Turkish for "bird island." It joins kuş, meaning bird, with ada, meaning island, plus the suffix -sı. Locals call the castle islet Güvercin Adası, or Pigeon Island, since güvercin means pigeon. The word kale, from Arabic, means castle or fortress, so the site is Güvercin Adası Kalesi. The English word "tower" comes from Latin turris, and Turkish borrowed kule from the same family. A flag is also a sign in semiotics. Its colors and shape stand for a nation, so even a tiny flag on the tower tells viewers who claims the place.
