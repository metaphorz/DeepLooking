A castle tower is built high so defenders can see far and shoot from above. Here the tower stands on the island's highest point, so it looks over the harbor and the sea approaches. Builders used thick masonry, usually local stone and mortar, to resist attack and weather. The flagpole on top is a slim cantilever. It must bend in the wind without snapping. The tower's white glow comes from floodlights aimed at the walls, which show off the structure at night. Engineers must pick lamps that resist salt spray and keep the lighting from glaring at ships. A causeway links the island to the mainland.
