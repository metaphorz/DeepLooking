The tower is a rectangular block, a box shape made of right angles, set on a hill that slopes up like a rough triangle. The wall below it is almost a long horizontal line, which makes the tower look like a small vertical mark above it. Trigonometry could estimate how tall the tower is. You would measure the angle up to its top and the distance across the water. If the angle were 3 degrees and the distance 500 meters, the height above the observer would be about 500 times tan(3°), which is roughly 26 meters. Perspective also makes the far tower seem tiny compared with the nearby piers.
