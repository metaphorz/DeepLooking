At dusk the white-lit tower becomes the brightest spot in the picture. Dark sky, dark sea, and dark trees surround it, so the eye goes straight to it. Artists call strong light-dark contrast chiaroscuro, and floodlighting makes a similar effect. The tower sits on top of the golden wall like a small crown, which gives the scene a clear focal point. The cool blue of the evening and the warm yellow of the wall are nearly opposite colors, so each makes the other look stronger. A photographer lining up this shot is using the same composition skills a painter would.
