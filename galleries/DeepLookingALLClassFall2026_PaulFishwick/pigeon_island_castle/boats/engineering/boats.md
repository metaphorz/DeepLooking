The two white boats are small passenger ferries designed for short harbor trips. Their hulls are usually made of steel or aluminum, with an upper deck, a wheelhouse for the captain, and an antenna mast for radar and radio. Diesel engines turn propellers or waterjets, and a flat, strong hull lets them stay stable with many passengers aboard. Notice how they are tied beside a concrete quay with dark rubber fenders. Those fenders work like shock absorbers, so the hulls do not scrape the wall when waves push them. Every part balances weight, strength, safety, and ease of docking.
