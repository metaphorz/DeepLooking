The two white vessels at the left edge of the harbor carry names with long histories. English boat comes from Old English bat, related to Old Norse beit, and likely goes back to a Germanic root for a hollowed or split log. Ferry comes from Old English ferian, meaning to carry or convey, which is why a ferry is defined by its job rather than its shape. In Turkish, a boat is a tekne, and a passenger ferry is a vapur, from the word for steam. Even the language of the harbor records changes in technology, from logs to steam to modern diesel engines.
