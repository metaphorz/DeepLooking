At dusk the two white boats glow against the dark water, a strong contrast that painters call value. Their clean white paint, horizontal window bands, and sloping bows give them a streamlined look that shipbuilders have long treated as a craft as well as a science. Boat design has been called an art of fair curves, because a smooth hull line is both pleasing to the eye and efficient in the water. Compare the sharp, bright boats with the soft golden walls of the castle behind them. The photographer's framing places the lit vessels at the edge, leading the eye across the harbor toward the fortress.
