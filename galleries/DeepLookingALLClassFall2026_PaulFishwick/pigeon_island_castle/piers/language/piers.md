The English word pier likely comes from Medieval Latin pera, meaning a support or bridge pillar, and it entered English through Anglo-Norman French. Sailors later used it for any structure jutting into water. Here, the white floating piers in Kusadasi harbor carry that old meaning. The town's name is tied to Turkish kus, 'bird', and ada, 'island', so Kusadasi means 'bird island', which fits the nearby Pigeon Island. Related harbor words like quay and jetty come from French too. Even the black round objects on the piers have a name, fenders, from 'fending off' a ship's hull.
