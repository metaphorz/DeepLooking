A photographer at dusk works much like a painter in the tonalist tradition, where a narrow range of blues and grays sets a quiet mood. Here the pale piers form two long horizontal bars that cut across the deep navy water, leading the eye toward the glowing golden castle walls. Warm yellow against cold blue is a classic complementary contrast, and it makes the fortress seem lit from within. The small yellow marker on the right pier repeats the castle color and balances the picture. The empty dark foreground adds calm and depth, like negative space in a drawing.
