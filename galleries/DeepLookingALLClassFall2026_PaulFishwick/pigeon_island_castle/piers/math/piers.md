The piers are close to straight line segments, and they run almost parallel to the horizon. That creates a strong horizontal rhythm, with the left pier about twice as long as the right one in the image. Perspective makes the farther parts of an object appear smaller, so measuring the apparent size of the fenders, the black bumpers spaced along the pier edge, shows how distance changes scale. Their repeated spacing is a simple pattern, close to an arithmetic sequence. The tall yellow marker adds a vertical line that meets the horizontal pier at nearly a right angle, giving a basic geometry of perpendicular lines.
