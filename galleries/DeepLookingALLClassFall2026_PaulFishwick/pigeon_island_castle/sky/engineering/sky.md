For harbor engineers and ship crews, the sky is working data. A calm, clear dusk sky like this one suggests stable weather and good visibility for docking. Ports such as Kusadasi rely on lights, like the glowing yellow marker on the right pier, because sky brightness fades quickly after sunset. Engineers design navigation lights with specific colors and heights so they stand out against the dark water and the blue sky. Cruise ships also use radar, GPS, and weather forecasts instead of depending on sky color alone, but officers still watch clouds and light by eye as a practical backup.
