The deep blue sky over Kusadasi is the kind of light English calls twilight, from Old English twi-, meaning 'two,' and light, so a 'half-light' between day and night. Turkish says gök for sky, and the word for dusk, alacakaranlık, means roughly 'mottled darkness.' French speakers call this hour entre chien et loup, 'between dog and wolf.' The Greek word ouranos, 'sky,' once named a god, and the Aegean coast here was Greek-speaking for thousands of years. Many languages use one word for both 'sky' and 'heaven,' showing how people have long linked what they see overhead with ideas about the sacred.
