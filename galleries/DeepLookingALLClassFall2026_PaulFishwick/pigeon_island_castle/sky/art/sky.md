This sky shows what photographers and painters call the blue hour, when the sun is below the horizon and the light turns deep, soft blue. The sky is a smooth gradient, lighter near the horizon and darker toward the top. Artists use this to create depth and calm. Painters such as the Romantic landscape artists, and later Impressionists like Monet, studied how evening light changes color. Here the cool blue makes the warm yellow glow on the castle walls stand out, a classic use of complementary colors. The nearly empty sky also gives the eye a place to rest and gives the scene a quiet, moody feeling.
