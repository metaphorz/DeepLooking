The water in this harbor belongs to the Aegean Sea, a name whose origin ancient writers explained with myth. One story says King Aegeus of Athens threw himself into it. Another links it to the Greek word aigides, meaning waves. Turkish calls this sea Ege Denizi, and deniz means sea. The town, Kusadasi, means Bird Island in Turkish, from kus (bird) and ada (island), which is why the fortress islet is nicknamed Pigeon Island. Sailors also developed a whole vocabulary for what we see here: a mole or pier, a breakwater, a fender, and a buoy. Each word names a tool for living beside moving water.
