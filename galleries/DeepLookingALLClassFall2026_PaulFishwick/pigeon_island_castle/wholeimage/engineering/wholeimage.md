Engineers designed everything here to meet the sea. The long concrete piers are built as floating or fixed platforms where ships can tie up, and the dark rounded fenders along their edges absorb the shock when a vessel touches them. The yellow beacon marks the pier's end so pilots can judge distance in low light. On the island, thick masonry walls follow the rock's outline, using the steep slope as part of the defense. The warm floodlights are a modern addition, aimed upward along the stone to show the wall's form while using little power.
