This photograph works like a painting built on color contrast. The deep blue of the sea and twilight sky surrounds the warm amber glow of the floodlit castle walls, and orange against blue is a classic complementary pairing that makes the fortress seem to glow. The island sits slightly off center, a strong horizontal band that divides sky and water. Smaller shapes, such as the white boats at left and the yellow beacon at right, balance each other across the frame. Photographers call this time just after sunset the blue hour, prized for its soft, even light and rich saturated tones.
