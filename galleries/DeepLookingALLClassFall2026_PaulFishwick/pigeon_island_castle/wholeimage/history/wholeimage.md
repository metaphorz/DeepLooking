The fortress on Pigeon Island dates mainly to the Ottoman period, and some sources place its construction in the 1600s. Kuşadası itself grew as a port near ancient Ephesus, and in the Middle Ages Venetian and Genoese merchants traded here. The small castle helped guard the harbor against pirates. In the 1950s a causeway linked the island to the mainland, and the site later became a park with a tower and cafes. Today, cruise ships like the one this photo was taken from bring visitors, continuing the port's long role as a gateway between the sea and the ruins of Ephesus.
