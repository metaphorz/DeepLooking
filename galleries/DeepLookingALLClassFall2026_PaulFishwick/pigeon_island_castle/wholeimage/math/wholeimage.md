Geometry organizes this scene. The horizon and the pier lines form long horizontals, while the tower and the yellow beacon provide short verticals. The sea fills roughly the lower half of the frame and the sky the upper part, close to a rule-of-thirds layout. The castle wall is a nearly straight line broken into rectangular segments, and the crenellated tower is a repeating pattern of solid and gap. Floating docks are modeled as rectangles with evenly spaced round fenders. The two piers, left and right, create a rough symmetry around the island, which anchors the center of the composition.
