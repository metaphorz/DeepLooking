The place name tells a story in two languages. In Turkish, Kuşadası means "bird island," from kuş (bird) and ada (island), with the suffix -sı marking possession. The islet in this photo is called Güvercin Adası, or Pigeon Island, from güvercin (pigeon). Some scholars link Kuşadası to the Italian Scala Nova or the Greek Neapolis, names from earlier ports on this coast. The yellow marker and the harbor signs belong to another code, the international language of navigation lights and buoys, where color and shape carry meaning for sailors who share no spoken language.
