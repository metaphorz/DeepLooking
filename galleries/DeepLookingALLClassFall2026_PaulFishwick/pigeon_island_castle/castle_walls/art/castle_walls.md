At dusk the castle walls become a painting in warm gold against a deep blue sky and sea. This is architectural lighting design, a craft where lamps are placed to reveal form and texture. Here, floodlights wash the stone so the crenellated top edge reads as a bright horizontal band, while the dark hill and trees behind it fall into silhouette. Artists call this strong light and dark contrast chiaroscuro. The orange and blue are near opposites on the color wheel, so each makes the other look stronger. The long, low wall also guides your eye from left to right across the quiet water, giving the photograph a calm, balanced composition.
