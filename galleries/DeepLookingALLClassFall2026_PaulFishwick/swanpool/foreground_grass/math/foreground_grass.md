How many blades of grass are in this lawn? You cannot count them all, but you can make a Fermi estimate, named after the physicist Enrico Fermi, who loved solving big questions with quick reasoning. Suppose you count 40 blades in a square 2 centimeters on each side. That is 10 blades per square centimeter, or 100,000 per square meter. If the lawn is about 50 meters by 30 meters, that is 1,500 square meters, so about 150 million blades. Change any guess and see how much the answer changes.
