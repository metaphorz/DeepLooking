The words grass, green and grow are thought to share one very old root, an ancient word that meant to grow or become green. Speakers of early English heard the connection that we might miss today. The word lawn has a different history. It comes from the Middle English launde, an open glade in a forest, borrowed from Old French. Only later did it come to mean the short, mown grass around houses, parks and clifftops, like the wide green space at the front of Pamela Gray's photo.
