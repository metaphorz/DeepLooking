Cornish place names along this coast describe the shape of the land. Porth means a cove or landing place, as in Porthleven and Porthcurno. Maen means stone. Maenporth, a sandy cove just south of Swanpool, means stone cove. Cornish miners also gave English the word killas, for the hard grey slaty rock found in these cliffs. Look at the low cliffs and the small white beach here. If you were naming them in Cornish, which features would you put in the name?
