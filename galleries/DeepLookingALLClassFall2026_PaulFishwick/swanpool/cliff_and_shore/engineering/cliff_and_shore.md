Engineers have several ways to protect a coast from the sea. Sea walls block waves with concrete. Groynes, low walls built out into the sea, trap sand so beaches stay wide and absorb the waves' energy. Piles of large boulders, called rock armour, break up waves before they reach the cliff. In some places, the choice is managed retreat: letting the coast move naturally and moving paths and buildings back. Look at the small beach and the cliffs here. Which method do you think would suit this shore?
