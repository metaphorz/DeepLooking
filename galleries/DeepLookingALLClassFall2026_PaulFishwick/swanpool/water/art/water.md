Henry Scott Tuke moved to Falmouth in 1885 and lived for many years in a cottage at Pennance, on the cliffs beside Swanpool. He painted outdoors, often from a boat, and became known for scenes of young swimmers, sailors and boats in bright summer water. Tuke worked hard to show how sunlight dances on the sea and on skin, using clear blues and greens. The quiet blue-grey bay in Pamela Gray's photo is part of the same stretch of coast he painted again and again.
