On the far side of Falmouth's harbor entrance stands St Anthony Lighthouse, built in 1835. Its light guides ships into the harbor and warns them away from the Manacles, a group of dangerous rocks to the south that have wrecked many ships. A lighthouse needs a bright lamp, a lens that bends the light into a strong beam, and a building that can stand up to storms and salt spray. The lighthouse also appeared as the home of Fraggle Rock in the British version of the children's TV show.
