Falmouth's name is a description: it is where the River Fal meets the sea. In Cornish, the old Celtic language of Cornwall, the town is called Aberfala. Aber means river mouth, the same word found in Welsh place names such as Aberystwyth, because Cornish and Welsh are close relatives. The English name and the Cornish name say the same thing in two languages. The calm bay in Pamela Gray's photo opens onto the waters around this river mouth, which made Falmouth one of the great harbors of Britain.
