Twice a day the sea in this bay rises and falls. The time from one high tide to the next is about 12 hours and 25 minutes, because the Moon moves a little further along its orbit each day. If you graph the water level over time, it makes a smooth, repeating curve close to a sine wave. Around Falmouth the difference between high and low water can be about 5 meters at spring tides. If high tide is at 9:00 in the morning, when should the next high tide be?
