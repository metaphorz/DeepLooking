Charles Causley, born in Launceston in 1917, is one of Cornwall's best-loved poets. He wrote in simple, musical language, often in the rhythms of old ballads. In his poem Eden Rock, he imagines his young parents having a picnic by a stream, and writes: The sky whitens as if lit by three suns. The line makes an ordinary bright day feel strange and almost holy. Look at the bright white clouds in Pamela's photo. How would you describe this Cornish sky in a single line of your own?
