This stretch of coast is part of the South West Coast Path, England's longest national trail, about 630 miles from Minehead in Somerset to Poole Harbour in Dorset. Much of it follows the routes coastguards once walked while watching for smugglers. Keeping it open takes constant work: steps cut into slopes, bridges over streams, gates and drainage to stop erosion, and sometimes moving the path inland when a cliff collapses. Walkers on the clifftop lawn in Pamela's photo are likely on or near this path.
