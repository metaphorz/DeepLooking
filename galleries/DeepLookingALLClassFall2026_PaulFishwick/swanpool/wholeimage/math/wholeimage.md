The segmentation map divides this photo into seven regions and measures how much of the image each one covers. Sky takes 38.1 percent, grass 25.1 percent, trees and bushes 22.2 percent, water 8.5 percent, fields 3.0 percent, cliff and shore 2.6 percent, and buildings and road 0.5 percent. Together they add up to 100. You could draw these as a pie chart, with each slice's angle equal to its percent times 3.6 degrees. How big would the sky's slice be? Notice how small the human-made part of the scene is.
