Just along the coast from Swanpool, Pendennis Castle stands on a headland at the entrance to Falmouth's harbor. King Henry VIII built it in the 1540s, with St Mawes Castle on the opposite shore, to guard against attack from France and Spain. During the English Civil War, in 1646, Pendennis held out for the King through a siege of about five months before its starving defenders surrendered. The castle was used for defense again in both World Wars. Falmouth's deep harbor was always worth protecting.
