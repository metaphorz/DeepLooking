Photographers and painters often imagine two lines across their picture and two lines down, dividing it into nine equal parts. Placing the horizon or the main subject along these lines, rather than in the center, often makes a picture feel balanced and alive. This is the rule of thirds. In Pamela Gray's photo, the bright sky fills the top part, the coast and trees sit across the middle, and the green lawn fills the lower third. The tall tree on the right closes the frame like a curtain at the side of a stage.
