One of the most common bushes on the Cornish coast is gorse, a spiny shrub with bright yellow flowers. It has so many names that you can tell where someone comes from by what they call it: gorse in much of England, furze in the southwest, and whin in the north and in Scotland. Gorse can flower in almost every month, which explains an old country saying: when gorse is out of blossom, kissing is out of fashion. Some of the dense green bushes along the clifftop in this photo may be gorse.
