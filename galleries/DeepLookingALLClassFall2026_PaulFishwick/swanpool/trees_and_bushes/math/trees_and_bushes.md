A tree is built from a simple rule repeated: a trunk splits into branches, each branch splits into smaller branches, and so on down to twigs. Each part looks like a small copy of the whole, a pattern mathematicians call a fractal. Leonardo da Vinci noticed that when a branch splits, the thickness of the new branches together roughly matches the thickness of the branch below. Look at the tall tree on the right side of Pamela's photo. Can you trace how many times a branch splits from the trunk to its outer tips?
