You can use a house to measure a cliff. If two objects are the same distance away, their sizes in a photo are in the same ratio as their real sizes. Suppose a house about 7 meters tall looks 1 centimeter high on your screen, and the cliff beside it looks 3 centimeters high. Then the cliff is about 3 times 7, or 21 meters tall. This idea comes from similar triangles, where shapes with the same angles have sides in the same ratio. Surveyors and astronomers use it all the time.
