An old rhyme says: By Tre, Pol and Pen, shall ye know the Cornishmen. Tre means a farm or settlement, Pol means a pool, and Pen means a head or headland. Thousands of Cornish place names start with these words, such as Tregony, Polperro and Penzance. Nearby Pennance Point is another example. The small white buildings in Pamela's photo sit in a landscape full of names like these, which keep the Cornish language alive on maps and road signs even where few people speak it.
