In 1863, a railway line opened from Truro to Falmouth, now called the Maritime Line. Suddenly, visitors from London and the cities of England could reach the Cornish coast in a day. Falmouth grew into a seaside resort, with large hotels, guest houses, and walks along the beaches and cliffs. Swanpool became a popular spot for bathing and boating. The road and buildings in Pamela's photo are part of that story, when the coast changed from a place of work, fishing and shipping into a place for holidays as well.
