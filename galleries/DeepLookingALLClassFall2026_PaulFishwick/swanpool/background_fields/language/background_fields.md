The English word field comes from Old English feld, which meant open, flat land without trees, related to the Dutch veld. Today we use it for a piece of farmland with hedges around it, almost the opposite of open land. In Cornish place names, the word for a field is park, which is why farm and field names in Cornwall often begin with Park or Parc. The bright green fields on the hill in Pamela's photo would each have had a name once, known to every farmer who worked them.
