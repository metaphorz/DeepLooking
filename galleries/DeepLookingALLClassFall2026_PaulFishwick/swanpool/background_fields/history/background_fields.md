In much of England, villages once farmed large open fields divided into strips, and many were only fenced into separate fields during the enclosure movement of the 1700s and 1800s. Cornwall was different. Many of its small fields were enclosed much earlier, some in the Middle Ages and some, in the far west, in prehistoric times. Historians can read the shapes of fields like a document: small, curved fields are often old, while large, straight-sided ones are often more recent. What shapes do you see in these fields?
