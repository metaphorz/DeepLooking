In England, farmland has long been measured in acres. An acre was once the area a team of oxen could plough in a day. It was later fixed as a strip one furlong long and one chain wide, 220 yards by 22 yards, which makes 4,840 square yards, about 0.4 hectares. Fields with curved hedges are not rectangles, so to find their area you can split them into triangles and rectangles and add the pieces. Pick one of the fields on the hill and sketch how you might divide it.
