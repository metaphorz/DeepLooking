The mountain road that climbs out of Geiranger toward Dalsnibba and the high plateau is usually closed in winter, when deep snow and avalanche danger make it unsafe. Each spring, crews use large snow blowers to cut through drifts several meters high, leaving tall walls of snow beside the road. Engineers reduce avalanche risk with fences that hold snow on the slopes, sheds built over the road, and controlled blasts that bring loose snow down when no one is near. The snow on these peaks is beautiful, but it is also a hazard that has to be managed.
