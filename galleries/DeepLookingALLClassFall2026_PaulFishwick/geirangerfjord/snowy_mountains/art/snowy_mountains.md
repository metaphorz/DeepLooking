Harald Sohlberg finished Winter Night in Rondane in 1914, after years of returning to the Rondane mountains in eastern Norway. The painting shows snowy peaks glowing under a deep blue night sky with a single bright star. In 1995 Norwegians voted it their national painting. Sohlberg simplified the mountains into clean shapes and let the cold blues and whites carry the feeling. Compare it with the white peaks in this photo: notice how the snow picks out the ridges and gullies, almost like a drawing in white on dark rock.
