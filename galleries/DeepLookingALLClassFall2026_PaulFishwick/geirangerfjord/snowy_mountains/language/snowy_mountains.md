English took the word ski directly from Norwegian, and it goes back to Old Norse skíð, a split piece of wood. Norwegian has many words for the snow and ice in these mountains. A fonn is a snowfield that lasts through summer, as in Folgefonna, a large ice cap in western Norway. A bre is a glacier, as in Jostedalsbreen, the largest glacier in mainland Europe, not far from here. Compare how many words you know for snow. People often name finely the things they live closely with.
