Air usually gets colder as you climb, by about 6.5 degrees Celsius for every 1,000 meters. Scientists call this the lapse rate. The mountains above Geiranger rise to well over 1,500 meters, while the fjord is at sea level. If it is 16 degrees in Geiranger village, you can estimate the temperature at 1,500 meters: 1.5 times 6.5 is about 10, so it would be about 6 degrees. Near the highest summits it can stay cold enough for snow to last into summer, which is why the peaks are still white.
