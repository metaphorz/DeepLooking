Weather observers do not just say cloudy. They divide the sky into eight parts and count how many are covered. Each part is one okta, so a clear sky is 0 oktas and a fully overcast sky is 8. In this photo, the clouds cover about three quarters of the visible sky, so an observer might report 6 oktas. The segmentation map says the sky takes up 17.4 percent of the image. Try it yourself: what fraction of that blue region is really cloud, and what fraction is open sky?
