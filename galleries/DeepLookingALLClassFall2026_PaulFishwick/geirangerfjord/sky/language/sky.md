The English word sky was borrowed from Old Norse, the language of the Vikings who sailed from fjords like this one. In Old Norse, ský meant cloud, not the blue dome above us. That fits this photo well: most of the sky here is covered by grey and white clouds. Modern Norwegian uses himmel for sky, a word related to English heaven. Other everyday English words came from Norse speakers too, such as window, from vindauga, meaning wind-eye, an opening in the roof that let smoke out and air in.
