The clouds over these mountains are part of Norway's power grid. Moist air from the Atlantic drops rain and snow on the high ground, and that water runs down into lakes and rivers. Engineers build dams and tunnels to store it and send it through turbines, and hydropower supplies about nine tenths of Norway's electricity. Western Norway is one of the wettest parts of Europe, which is why it has so many power stations. The steep drop from mountain to fjord gives the falling water extra energy.
