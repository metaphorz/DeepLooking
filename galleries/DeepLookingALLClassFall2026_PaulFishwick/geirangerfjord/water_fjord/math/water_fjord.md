How long is Norway's coast? The answer depends on the size of your ruler. Measured in a smooth line, the mainland coast is about 2,500 kilometers. If you follow every fjord, it is more than 25,000 kilometers, and counting all the islands it is about 100,000 kilometers. The shorter the ruler, the more bends you catch, so the length keeps growing. This is the coastline paradox, and it led mathematicians to the idea of fractals. A fjord like this one is one of the bends that makes the Norwegian coast so long.
