Fjord is one of the few Norwegian words used all over the world. It comes from Old Norse fjǫrðr, a place you travel across, and it is related to English ford and Scottish firth. The name Geiranger also holds a word for a fjord: the ending comes from Old Norse angr, a bay or inlet, as in Hardanger and Stavanger. The first part is usually linked to geirr, spear. So the name of the village itself describes the long, narrow arm of water you see at its foot.
