One of Norway's most loved paintings, Bridal Procession on the Hardangerfjord, was made in 1848 by two artists working together. Hans Gude painted the fjord, mountains and sky, and Adolph Tidemand painted the people in a line of rowing boats carrying a bride to church. The calm water reflects the mountains like a mirror. The painting celebrated rural Norwegian life at a time when Norway was building its national identity. Notice how the still water in Lou Ann's photo also turns the fjord into a smooth, dark ribbon between the slopes.
