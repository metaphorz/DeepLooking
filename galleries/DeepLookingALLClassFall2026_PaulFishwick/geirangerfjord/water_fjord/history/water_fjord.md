In 2005, the Geirangerfjord and the Nærøyfjord were named a UNESCO World Heritage Site, together called the West Norwegian Fjords. UNESCO chose them as some of the finest examples of fjord landscape on Earth, with sheer rock walls, waterfalls, and old farms. The listing helped protect the area, but also brought more visitors. Geiranger village has only a few hundred residents, yet in summer it welcomes many cruise ships and hundreds of thousands of tourists. Lou Ann Suits's view shows both sides of that story: wild nature and the ships in the water.
