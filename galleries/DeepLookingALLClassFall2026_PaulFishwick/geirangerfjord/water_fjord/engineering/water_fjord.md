Not far from Geiranger, on the neighboring Sunnylvsfjorden, a huge section of mountainside at Åknes is slowly cracking away, a few centimeters each year. If it collapsed into the fjord, it could set off a tsunami that would reach villages like Geiranger within minutes. Engineers have covered the slope with sensors, GPS stations and laser instruments that measure every movement. Their data feed a warning system with sirens, so people in the fjord villages could reach higher ground in time. Fjords are beautiful, but their steep walls can be dangerous.
