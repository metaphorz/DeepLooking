In a photo, things farther away look smaller, and the rule is simple: twice as far looks half as big. This is why the road in the valley appears thick near the bottom of the image and thin near the fjord, and why the cruise ships look like small white dots. Artists use the mathematics of perspective, with lines that meet at a vanishing point, to draw scenes that look real. Try following the valley from the bottom of the photo toward the fjord. Notice how the land seems to shrink and squeeze together with distance.
