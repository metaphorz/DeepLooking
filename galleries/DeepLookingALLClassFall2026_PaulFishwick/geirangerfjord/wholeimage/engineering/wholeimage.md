Norway has 18 National Scenic Routes, roads chosen for their landscapes and fitted with viewpoints, rest stops and railings designed by architects. The Geiranger–Trollstigen route runs right through this area. Its viewpoints, such as the platforms overlooking the Geirangerfjord, are built of steel, glass, concrete and stone to sit lightly on the cliffs while holding crowds of visitors safely. Engineers must anchor them to rock, plan for snow loads and ice, and keep them safe in strong wind. A high, open view like this one depends on that careful design.
