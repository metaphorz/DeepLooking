When the main glacier carved this fjord, smaller glaciers in side valleys could not cut as deep. When the ice melted, those side valleys were left high on the walls of the main valley, ending in a sudden drop. Geologists call this a hanging valley, and its stream falls straight down the cliff as a waterfall. The Geirangerfjord is famous for them, including the Seven Sisters, with streams that fall about 250 meters. Look for the thin white lines of water on the slopes in Lou Ann Suits's photo.
