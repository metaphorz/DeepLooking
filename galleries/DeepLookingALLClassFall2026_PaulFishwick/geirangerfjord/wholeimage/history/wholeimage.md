Farms in valleys like this one were small, steep and hard to work, and land was scarce. Between 1825 and 1925, about 800,000 Norwegians emigrated to North America, one of the largest shares of any country's population. Many settled in Minnesota, Wisconsin and the Dakotas, where they built farms, churches and newspapers in Norwegian. The first organized group sailed in 1825 on a small ship called the Restauration. Abandoned farms on Norway's fjords, and Norwegian names across the American Midwest, are both part of this story.
