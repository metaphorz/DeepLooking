The lower slopes in this photo are covered with forest, mostly birch, pine and spruce, but higher up the trees thin out and give way to rock, grass and snow. That boundary is the tree line. Above it, summers are too short and cold for trees to grow. In western Norway the tree line is often found around 800 to 1,000 meters. Strong wind, thin soil and heavy snow can push it lower. Compare the dark green lower slopes with the paler ground near the white peaks.
