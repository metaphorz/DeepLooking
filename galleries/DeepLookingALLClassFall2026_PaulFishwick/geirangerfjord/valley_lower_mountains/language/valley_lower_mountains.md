The Norwegian word dal means valley, and it is related to the English word dale. On the green slopes of valleys like this one, farmers once moved their cows and goats up to summer mountain farms. A summer farm is called a seter or a støl. There, dairymaids milked the animals and made butter and cheese, including the brown cheese Norwegians still eat. Farming families moved with the seasons, which is called transhumance. Look for small clearings and buildings on the slopes, where grass replaces the forest.
