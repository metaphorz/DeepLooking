In 1842, J. C. Dahl painted View from Stalheim, looking down a deep valley in western Norway with a river, farms and steep mountain walls. It is one of the best-known Norwegian landscapes of the Romantic period, when painters wanted to show the grandeur of nature and the small place of people within it. Lou Ann Suits's photo has a similar design: a high viewpoint, mountain walls on both sides, and a valley floor that leads the eye into the distance. Find the road and the farms. They give the scene its sense of scale.
