Big ships have usually burned heavy fuel oil or diesel, and their exhaust can hang in a narrow fjord on calm days. Norway's parliament decided that ships in its World Heritage fjords must become zero-emission, starting with smaller vessels. Engineers are answering with ferries and tour boats that run on large batteries charged from the shore, and with ships designed for hydrogen or other clean fuels. Norway already has some of the world's first electric car ferries. Cruise ships like the ones in this photo are a harder problem because they are so big.
