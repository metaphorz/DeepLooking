From high above, it is hard to judge distance. A ship of known size can act as a ruler and give you a sense of scale. A large cruise ship can be about 300 meters long. Suppose the fjord near the ship looks about four ship lengths wide. Then the fjord is about 4 times 300, or 1,200 meters, across. You can use the same idea to estimate the length of the visible fjord or the height of the cliffs. It is a quick estimate, not an exact measurement, but it is a powerful habit.
