Tourists began sailing into the Norwegian fjords in the late 1800s, aboard steamships that carried them to see waterfalls and mountains. German Emperor Wilhelm II loved these cruises and sailed to Norway on his yacht Hohenzollern almost every summer from 1889 until 1914, and Geiranger was among his stops. Hotels and roads followed the visitors. The cruise ships in Lou Ann's photo continue that tradition, carrying thousands of passengers on a single visit to a village of only a few hundred people.
