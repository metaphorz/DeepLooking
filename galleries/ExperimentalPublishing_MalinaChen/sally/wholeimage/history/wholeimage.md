The Saigon Notre-Dame Cathedral, or Nhà Thờ Đức Bà in Vietnamese, was built between 1863 and 1880 by French colonists. Its primary purpose was to serve the growing Catholic community while showcasing European architectural elegance in Vietnam. It's a piece of living history expressing French colonial elegance with red-brick facade. The red bricks was imported all the way from Marseille, France. Until this day, it remains a center of cultural and religious significance.
