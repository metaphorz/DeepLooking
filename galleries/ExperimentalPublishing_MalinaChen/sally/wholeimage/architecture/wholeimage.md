The Saigon Notre Dame Cathedral was influenced by the Neo-Romanesque architectural style. This architectural style emerged in the mid-19th century, drawing inspiration from Romanesque architecture of the 11th and 12th centuries. The Neo-Romanesque buildings featured iconic yet simplified arches and windows compared to other historical architecture counterparts. All materials, including cement, steel, and screws, were imported from France. The cathedral remains unpainted to showcase the natural color of the bricks.
