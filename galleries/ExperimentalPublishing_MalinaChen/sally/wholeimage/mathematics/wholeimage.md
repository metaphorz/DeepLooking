The cathedral's design is a testament to the precision of 19th-century French engineering, using mathematical principles to ensure both aesthetic beauty and structural longevity. The twin bell towers rise to 36.6 meters, but with the addition of the iron spires in 1885, they reach a precise height of 57.6 meters, maintaining a strict vertical symmetry that dominates the skyline of District 1. The foundation was mathematically engineered to support ten times the total weight of the structure. This level of over-engineering has allowed the cathedral to remain stable for nearly 150 years without a single crack.
