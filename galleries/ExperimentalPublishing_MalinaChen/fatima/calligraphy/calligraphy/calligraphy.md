The large-scale calligraphic text rendered on this billboard publication is in Urdu, Pakistan's national language. It reads انکہی کہانیاں — Ankahi Kahaniyan — which translates to Untold Stories in English. The choice of Urdu script is itself a declaration: the stories held by these walls have a language, and it is not the one that erased them.
