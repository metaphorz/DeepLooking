title: Memory
poet: Fatima Siddique

I grew up in Karachi. Knowing that we stand on the verge of losing these buildings, structures that connect us to our culture, to the struggles of those who came before, to the majesty of their era. It feels like a particular kind of grief. This is not just architectural loss. It is the slow erasure of a people's memory of themselves.
