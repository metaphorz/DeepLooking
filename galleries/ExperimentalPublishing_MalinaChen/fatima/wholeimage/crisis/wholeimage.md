Across Pakistan, heritage buildings are disappearing at an alarming rate. In a recent episode of The Pakistan Experience, architect and heritage consultant Marvi Mazhar joined host Shezad Ghias in conversation with renowned architect, urban planner, and activist Arif Hasan. Hasan argued that what Pakistan urgently needs is urban land reform, and that existing structures should not be demolished without first understanding their nature and quality. He noted that many well-built buildings from the 1950s and '60s are currently being torn down. Mazhar pressed further, questioning how seriously the country is investing in archival processes and the active preservation of the city's history.
