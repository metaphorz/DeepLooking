poet: Paul Monaco

Knowing this pictograph sits in the Council Rocks area of the Dragoon Mountains makes me experience it from a new perspective. The site already carries a sense of presence, and I can feel the history created in the location. An eerie sense of struggle and pride comes over me. Massive boulders etched by wind, and the quietness engulfs the atmosphere. Standing in front of these faint red marks, the weight of a place that has held human gatherings, stories, and movement for thousands of years. The pictographs aren't just isolated symbols; they are part of the landscape's memory. The rock face seems to hold onto gestures made long before modern borders or categories existed. Looking at it makes me aware of how small and temporary my own aspects.
