With my observation, I get a conflicting aspect of chaos and comfort. The alternating patterns bring the chaotic nature of the subject. The density and warmth of the colors exhibits comfort. The subject pushes and pulls on the feelings. The evenly spaced colored lines bring partial order among the chaos of black and white jagged patterns. I also get an African cultural vibe from the piece.
