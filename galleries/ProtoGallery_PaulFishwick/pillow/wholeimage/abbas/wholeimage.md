Tribal art, loads of colors.

Gives me a sense that the pillow might be from an African country, because they do have such art in Swahili culture. Lots of orange which is quite appealing to the eye. Not at all color-blind friendly. The dots and dashes might be some sort of message, morse code. Why are the arrows on the pillow only moving upwards? The pillow looks a bit old/dirty, colour has faded away. Does the middle band signify a rainbow — is the message something related to rain? Because of the severe shortage of water/drought conditions in the African Peninsula.
