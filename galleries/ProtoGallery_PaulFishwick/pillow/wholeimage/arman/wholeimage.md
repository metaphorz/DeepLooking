The first thing that hits me is the tension between the handmade and the structured.

The black symbols in the center look like a secret language — they remind me of cuneiform or ancient tally marks.

There's a digital feel to the rows, almost like a binary code, but the shaky lines prove a human hand was there. I also keep looking at the clash of the background. The pillow is bold and loud, sitting on top of that dark, busy floral fabric. It feels like two different cultures or eras are sitting on top of each other. It makes the pillow feel like a traveler, something brought from a specific place into a domestic, everyday setting. It feels heavy with history, but it's just an object for a couch.

That contrast is what sticks with me: The Monumental vs. The Mundane.
