Residuals (SSE)

Five objects trace a descending arc from upper-left to lower-right. Bryson called it "precisely logarithmic" then "hyperbolic." Zorn noted the contradiction and fitted multiple families.

With 5 data points (including the melon slice), only the cubic remains near-exact. The other fits are genuinely overdetermined.

J. Sánchez Cotán, Quince, Cabbage, Melon, and Cucumber, c. 1602. Oil on canvas, 68.9 × 84.5 cm. San Diego Museum of Art.
