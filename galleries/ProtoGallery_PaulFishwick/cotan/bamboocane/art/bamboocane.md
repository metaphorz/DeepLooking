title: The Vertical Axis — Compositional Anchor

The bamboo cane serves a critical compositional role in Cotán's painting. It provides the only strong vertical line in an image dominated by the hyperbolic curve of hanging objects and the horizontal plane of the stone ledge.

This vertical element anchors the left side of the composition and creates a visual bridge between the upper hanging zone and the lower ledge. The cane's bright green contrasts sharply with the dark void behind it, making it one of the most visually distinct elements despite its slender form.

Cotán positions the cane at the exact point where the descending curve of fruits reaches the ledge — it marks the transition between suspension and rest, between the ethereal hanging objects and the grounded, earthly ones. This placement is too precise to be accidental.
