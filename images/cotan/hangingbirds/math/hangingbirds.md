title: Pendulum Physics — The Geometry of Hanging
url: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pendulum
keyword: pendulum

Each hanging bird forms a simple pendulum — a mass suspended from a fixed point by a string. The period of oscillation T = 2π√(L/g), where L is the string length and g is gravitational acceleration. For strings approximately 15-20 cm long, the natural period would be about 0.8 seconds.

The two birds hang at slightly different heights and string lengths, creating an asymmetric pair. This asymmetry is compositionally deliberate — Cotán avoids perfect symmetry throughout the painting, preferring the dynamic tension of near-symmetry.

The goldfinch weighs roughly 14-19 grams, the sparrow 24-40 grams. Despite this weight difference, both hang nearly vertically — the gravitational force overwhelms any lateral perturbation at this scale. The center of mass of each bird hangs directly below its suspension point, following the principle that stable equilibrium minimizes potential energy.
